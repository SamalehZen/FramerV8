## 2025-10-24 - Accessible Interactive Overlays and Carousel Navigation
**Learning:** Carousels and accordion components frequently suffer from missing keyboard control patterns. Simply relying on mouse hover or touch interactions fails standard accessibility testing. By introducing WAI-ARIA roles (`role="tablist"`, `role="tab"`, `role="group"`, `aria-roledescription="carousel"`, `role="region"`) paired with explicit `onKeyDown` navigation handlers supporting Arrow keys, we can deliver seamless screen reader-friendly and keyboard-accessible micro-interactions.
**Action:** When designing custom components (e.g., carousels, tabs, accordions), ensure that keyboard shortcuts (like arrow keys) are fully supported, focus-visible states are beautifully integrated, and semantic ARIA states correspond perfectly to their visibility status.
