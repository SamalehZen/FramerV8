## 2025-05-18 - Accordion ARIA & Focus States
**Learning:** Accordion components need full ARIA attributes (`aria-expanded`, `aria-controls`, `role="region"`, `aria-labelledby`) and explicit focus-visible rings to ensure seamless keyboard navigation and screen reader support.
**Action:** Always include matching `id` and `aria-controls`/`aria-labelledby` linkages and `focus-visible:ring-2` styles when making custom interactive accordion elements.
