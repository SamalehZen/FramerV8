## 2026-11-09 - Card Accordion Accessibility and Focus Rings
**Learning:** In card-styled accordion lists, placing focus indicators directly on an inner full-width trigger button often results in awkward internal borders or clipped rings. Using `focus-within:ring-2 focus-within:ring-navy` on the outer card container combined with `focus:outline-none` on the button trigger cleanly encloses the full card component without clipping, alongside standard W3C ARIA accordion attributes (`aria-expanded`, `aria-controls`, `role="region"`, `aria-labelledby`).
**Action:** When styling card-based accordion items or collapsible panels, delegate focus rings to the container card via `focus-within` and ensure complete W3C ARIA accordion pairings (`aria-expanded`/`aria-controls` with `role="region"`/`aria-labelledby`).
