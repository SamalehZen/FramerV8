## 2025-05-18 - Card-Styled Accordion Focus and ARIA Structure
**Learning:** In card-styled accordion lists where the toggle `<button>` is wrapped in a rounded container card with padding, placing keyboard focus ring utilities (`focus-visible:ring-*`) directly on the `<button>` clips or creates an awkward inner outline disjointed from the card boundary. Using `focus-within:ring-2 focus-within:ring-navy` on the outer card wrapper alongside `focus:outline-none` on the button trigger produces a clean, natural focus ring enclosing the entire card component while maintaining keyboard accessibility.
**Action:** Pair `focus-within:ring-2` on card wrappers with `focus:outline-none` on nested trigger buttons, and always wire W3C ARIA accordion relationships (`aria-expanded`, `aria-controls`, `role="region"`, `aria-labelledby`).
