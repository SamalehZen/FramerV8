## 2025-05-18 - Accordion Keyboard Focus & ARIA Controls
**Learning:** Accordion components need full WAI-ARIA controls (`aria-expanded`, `aria-controls`, `role="region"`, `aria-labelledby`) and high-contrast focus rings (`focus-visible:ring-2 focus-visible:ring-navy focus-visible:ring-inset`) for proper screen reader and keyboard navigation without layout shifts.
**Action:** Always include complete ARIA accessibility attributes and inset focus styles on custom interactive disclosure / accordion items.
