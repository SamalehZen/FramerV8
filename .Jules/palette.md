## 2025-05-18 - Accordion Keyboard Focus and ARIA Region Patterns
**Learning:** In card-styled accordion lists with animated expandable content, applying focus rings directly to the trigger button inside a padded card results in clipped or disjointed outlines that exclude the expanded answer region. Using `focus-within:ring-2 focus-within:ring-navy` on the outer card container (paired with `focus:outline-none` on the button trigger) ensures the focus ring seamlessly encloses the entire active card component without visual clipping, while standard W3C ARIA relationships (`aria-expanded`, `aria-controls`, `role="region"`, `aria-labelledby`) ensure complete screen reader accessibility.
**Action:** When designing or refactoring accordion components with container cards, apply `focus-within` on the card container to preserve border-radius styling and encapsulate the trigger and region cleanly, ensuring both keyboard visual cues and screen reader semantics are honored.
