## 2025-08-11 - Accordion ARIA & Focus Alignment
**Learning:** Accordion buttons and interactive sliders in custom Tailwind sites often lack focus-visible rings and ARIA relationship attributes. To make these keyboard-navigable without inducing layout shifts (caused by adding outline borders or padding shifts), we can use a negative horizontal margin combined with matching horizontal padding (`px-3 -mx-3`) on the button. This expands the focus ring's visual container slightly outward, perfectly aligning the interior text contents while presenting a beautiful, roomy focus boundary that matches the container's roundness.
**Action:** Always verify custom buttons in menus, accordions, and sliders have both ARIA state controls (`aria-expanded`/`aria-controls`/`role="region"`) and negative-offset `focus-visible:ring` feedback.
