## 2025-05-18 - Accordion ARIA & Focus-Visible Ring Inset
**Learning:** Interactive accordion elements require proper ARIA attributes (`aria-expanded`, `aria-controls`, `role="region"`, `aria-labelledby`) and high-contrast visible keyboard focus indicators (`focus-visible:ring-2 focus-visible:ring-navy focus-visible:ring-inset`) for full accessibility compliance without layout shifts.
**Action:** Apply `aria-*` controls and `focus-visible:ring-inset` on full-width rounded button triggers inside padded containers.
