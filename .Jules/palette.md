## 2025-05-20 - FAQ Accordion Accessibility & Focus Outline
**Learning:** Interactive accordion components require proper WAI-ARIA pattern attributes (`aria-expanded`, `aria-controls`, `role="region"`, `aria-labelledby`) and visible high-contrast focus rings (`focus-visible:ring-2 focus-visible:ring-navy focus-visible:ring-inset`) for seamless screen reader and keyboard accessibility.
**Action:** Always include semantic WAI-ARIA attributes and high-contrast `focus-visible` focus styles when creating or updating custom accordion/disclosure elements.
